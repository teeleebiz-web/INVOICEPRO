Overview

BizPilot and InvoicePro are mobile business management apps built for independent professionals. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when using our apps:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and business name
  • Business data — client records, proposals, contracts, invoices, and documents you enter into the app
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe; we never store card numbers
  • Profile information — your business logo and profile details

We also collect basic usage data to improve the apps, such as which features are used most often.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide our app features — managing your clients, sending proposals and invoices, processing payments, and delivering AI-powered assistance. We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted database platform with enterprise-grade encryption at rest and in transit. Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

Third-Party Services

Our apps use the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase — database and file storage
  • Stripe — payment processing and subscriptions
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Anthropic — AI assistant features (BizPilot)
  • BoldSign — electronic signatures (BizPilot)

Each service has its own privacy policy governing their use of data.

AI Features

BizPilot AI assistant uses your business data — client names, invoice amounts, proposal statuses — to answer your questions and assist with tasks. This data is sent to Anthropic API to generate responses. We do not use your business data to train AI models.

SMS Communications

If you provide a phone number and consent to SMS, we may send you notifications related to your account and business activity. You can opt out at any time by contacting us or updating your app settings. We do not share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is scheduled for permanent deletion within 30 days. You can request immediate deletion by contacting us at the address below.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To delete your account, go to Settings inside the app. To request a copy of your data or for any other privacy request, contact us directly.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
